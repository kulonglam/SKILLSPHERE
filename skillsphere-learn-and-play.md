## SKILLSPHERE | LEARN & PLAY

### Practical Skills for Every Stage of Life • Learn. Play. Apply. Grow.

## THE OPPORTUNITY

Skillsphere Learn & Play is a proposed gamified digital learning platform that will make practical life-skills education engaging, accessible and continuous for children, young people and adults. Building on Skillsphere’s experiential camps, mentorship and school engagements, it will connect learning at camps, at home, in schools and within communities.

Skillsphere has seen children return from camp discussing budgeting, financial-management tools and business-planning concepts that some parents have not encountered. Parents have expressed interest in learning these skills too. Meanwhile, limited internet access, data costs and device availability restrict participation in many upcountry communities. The proposed platform responds to both the demand for lifelong learning and the need for inclusive delivery.

## ONE WEBSITE, TWO LEARNING EXPERIENCES

### SKILLSPHERE JUNIOR | AGES 6–17

A colourful, animated, game-based experience with age-appropriate pathways for ages 6–9, 10–13 and 14–17. Learners explore interactive stories, quizzes, challenges and real-world simulations, earning points, badges and certificates as they progress.

### SKILLSPHERE ACADEMY | AGES 18+

A clean, professional yet visually engaging environment offering short, self-paced courses, illustrations, interactive exercises, practical assignments and simulations. An introductory assessment will help adults choose learning pathways that match their existing knowledge and goals.

## LEARNING THAT TRANSLATES INTO ACTION

Core subjects will include financial literacy, entrepreneurship, digital literacy and online safety, leadership, communication, decision-making, problem-solving, personal development and career readiness. A child might manage a virtual allowance; an adult might build a Business Model Canvas or complete a cash-flow simulation. Learning will focus on applying knowledge—not simply watching videos.

## BEYOND INDIVIDUAL LEARNERS

### SKILLSPHERE CLUBS IN SCHOOLS

Structured digital modules, team quizzes, practical challenges and school competitions will reinforce learning through facilitated club sessions.

### FAMILY LEARNING

Linked but separate parent and child accounts will support age-appropriate challenges, such as family saving goals and household budgeting exercises.

## ACCESS FOR LEARNERS WITH LIMITED INTERNET

The platform will be designed as a mobile-first Progressive Web App (PWA): a website that can be installed on supported phones, tablets and computers. With deliberate offline-first development, users will be able to download selected lessons, quizzes and lightweight simulations while connected, then study offline. Progress will be saved locally and synchronised when connectivity returns. Offline use will require an initial download and sufficient device storage; real-time competitions, payments and some features will still need internet.

## REACHING UPCOUNTRY AND UNDERSERVED COMMUNITIES

Low-data illustrations, compressed media, short audio-supported lessons and shared-device options will reduce barriers to access. In later phases, Skillsphere will explore school and community offline learning hubs: local servers and Wi-Fi networks that distribute preloaded content without continuous internet. These hubs will require equipment, electricity and periodic updates, but could extend access to schools with weak connectivity.

## MEASURING TRAINING, PROGRESS AND IMPACT

A built-in monitoring, evaluation and learning dashboard will connect digital learning with Skillsphere’s camps, school clubs and outreach activities. Different views will serve learners, parents, authorised facilitators, school leaders and Skillsphere management.

**Participation** Unique registered learners, active users, participants trained, attendance, course enrolments, school clubs and geographic reach.

**Learning outcomes** Baseline and follow-up assessment scores, modules completed, skills demonstrated, completion rates, certificates and returning learners.

**Programme performance** Learning activity uptake, offline synchronisation, school participation and course engagement.

**Management and funder reporting** Aggregated impact summaries, downloadable reports, institutional licences, subscription trends and progress against programme targets.

“Participants trained” will be counted using defined completion criteria, rather than treating all registrations as training outcomes. Access to children’s information will be restricted by role, with appropriate guardian consent, safeguarding and data-protection measures.

## SUSTAINABLE BUSINESS MODEL

Skillsphere envisages Junior subscriptions for families, Academy subscriptions for adults, combined family plans, school licences and sponsored access supported by corporate and development partners. Mobile-money payments will be explored for local convenience. Pricing and willingness to pay will be tested during pilots rather than assumed.

## DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP

**01 Design and prototype** Map curricula and user journeys; build Junior and Academy interfaces and a demonstrable learning activity.

**02 Offline-capable MVP** Build learner accounts, initial modules, quizzes, simulations, offline storage/synchronisation and a basic impact dashboard.

**03 Pilot and validate** Test with children, parents and schools; measure usability, engagement, learning gains and willingness to pay.

**04 Launch and expand** Add subscriptions, school dashboards, more learning pathways and progressively broader rural-access solutions.

## OUR STARTING POINT

Skillsphere already delivers experiential learning programmes and develops its own educational content and quiz questions. Its technology-enabled quizzes currently run on a partner’s software. Learn & Play is a proposed independent platform, not yet a live Skillsphere-owned product. The initial pilot will establish evidence of use, learning outcomes and demand before scaling.

## THE VISION

To make practical skills development a continuous part of everyday life—empowering children and adults to learn, practise and grow at their own pace, wherever they live.
